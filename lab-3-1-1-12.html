Task 1 - Understand the problem

Context

Your task: Write a program that determines if a year is a leap year or common year.

Leap Year Rules (Gregorian Calendar):

  • If the year is NOT divisible by 4 → common year
  • Otherwise, if the year is NOT divisible by 100 → leap year
  • Otherwise, if the year is NOT divisible by 400 → common year
  • Otherwise → leap year

Key concepts:

  • if/elif/else : multiple condition branches
  • % (modulo) : remainder after division (divisibility check)
  • != : not equal operator
  • Order matters: check conditions from most restrictive first
