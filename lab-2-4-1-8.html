Task 1 - Understand the conversion

Context

The conversion factor: 1 mile = 1.61 kilometers

Formulas to implement:

  • Miles to kilometers: miles * 1.61
  • Kilometers to miles: kilometers / 1.61

The round() function: round(value, decimals) rounds a number to specified decimal places.
